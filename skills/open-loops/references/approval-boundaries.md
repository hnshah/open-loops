# Approval boundaries

Open Loops v0 is primarily a judgment and preparation skill.

## May do without external approval

Within the already-authorized workspace, the agent may

- search and read sources
- analyze evidence
- locate files
- draft replies
- prepare meeting briefs
- research answers
- suggest calendar times
- assemble a review-ready next step

## Requires explicit approval

Do not do these merely because the next step seems obvious

- send email or chat messages
- accept or decline invitations
- create or change calendar events
- publish content
- update CRM, project, or ticket records
- create commitments on behalf of the user
- delete or archive records
- make purchases or financial actions
- change permissions or access scope

## Approval should be specific

Approval for one action does not imply approval for future similar actions.

If the user says "send that," the approved action is the specific prepared message unless the user clearly grants a broader scope.

## Routine mode

A recurring scan may run without repeated approval if the user explicitly schedules it, but the scan still does not gain permission to send, publish, schedule, or mutate external systems.

Routine ownership begins with noticing, not acting.
