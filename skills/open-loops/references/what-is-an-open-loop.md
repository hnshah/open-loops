# What is an open loop

Use this reference when a candidate is hard to classify.

An open loop is an exchange, commitment, dependency, decision, or upcoming event for which meaningful work remains unresolved.

## I owe

Surface when the user made an explicit or strongly implied commitment and no valid closure exists.

Examples

- "I'll send you the deck tomorrow."
- "I'll introduce you to Maya."
- "Give me until Friday."
- A meeting assigns the user a concrete action.

Do not require the exact phrase "I will." Strongly implied ownership can be enough when the context is clear.

## Waiting on

Surface when someone else committed to deliver something, the expected time has arrived or passed, and no delivery or valid change was found.

Examples

- revised proposal promised by Friday
- requested introduction accepted but not made
- contract promised tomorrow

Do not nag before the reasonable expected time unless the dependency is already blocking something important.

## Response expected

An explicit promise is not required when social or work context clearly calls for a response.

Examples

- direct question
- meaningful introduction where the other person replied
- customer escalation
- approval request

Ignore rhetorical questions and FYIs.

## Decision

Surface when the conversation reached a real decision point, a choice is expected, and no decision or deferral was found.

A vague discussion of possibilities is not a decision loop.

## Follow-up

Surface when a concrete later interaction was established.

Examples

- "Let's reconnect after your launch next Thursday."
- "Check back with me in September."

Ignore casual language such as "we should grab coffee sometime" unless the user has explicitly taught the skill that this relationship class matters.

## Prepare

Surface when an upcoming event plus supporting evidence implies preparation.

The calendar event alone is usually insufficient. Look for evidence such as

- "bring the churn breakdown"
- "we will review the demo"
- "please come with a recommendation"

## Dependency

Surface when progress is blocked because an expected action or piece of information is missing.

Be clear about who owns the blocking step.

## What is not an open loop

Suppress by default

- informational messages
- FYIs
- completed commitments
- requests the user declined
- speculative ideas with no implied action
- marketing email and routine notifications
- conversations that naturally concluded
- duplicates of the same obligation
- old obligations made obsolete by later context
- weak social niceties
- work already captured and completed elsewhere

The skill should not turn ordinary communication into a burden.
